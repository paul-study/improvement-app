# LifeLift - Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in your web browser to start using LifeLift!

## Features Overview

### 🗣️ Self-Talk
1. Read the daily affirmation displayed
2. Click "New Affirmation" for a different one
3. Create your own affirmations in the input field
4. Your custom affirmations are saved automatically

### 💪 Motivation
- Click "Next Quote" to get inspiring quotes from notable figures
- Each quote is randomly selected from a curated collection

### 🧘 Daily Exercises
1. Click "Start" on any exercise card
2. A modal will open with instructions and a timer
3. Click "Start" in the modal to begin the countdown
4. Exercise completion is tracked in your statistics

### 🎯 Goal Setting
1. Enter your goal in the text field
2. Select a category (Health, Career, Learning, etc.)
3. Optionally set a deadline
4. Click "Add Goal"
5. Check the box when you complete a goal
6. Delete goals you no longer need

### 🏆 Achievements
- View your statistics at the top (goals, exercises, streak)
- Log daily achievements in the input field
- All achievements are saved with dates

### 📚 Skills Development
1. Enter a skill name or click a suggested skill
2. Choose your current level
3. Click "Track Skill"
4. Click "Practice" to log a practice session (+5% progress)
5. Watch your progress bar grow!

### 🌱 Habit Building
1. Enter a habit you want to build
2. Click "Start Challenge"
3. Click on each day number as you complete it
4. Days turn green when completed
5. Track your progress percentage
6. Try to maintain your streak!

### 📖 Resources
Browse curated lists of:
- Recommended books
- Helpful podcasts
- Useful tools
- Daily practice suggestions

## Data Storage

All your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage:
- Your goals, achievements, skills, and habits persist between sessions
- Data stays on your device only (not sent to any server)
- Clear your browser data to reset the app

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be Consistent**: Visit daily to build momentum
2. **Start Small**: Don't overwhelm yourself with too many goals
3. **Track Progress**: Log achievements regularly to stay motivated
4. **Review Weekly**: Check your stats and adjust as needed
5. **Celebrate Wins**: No matter how small, acknowledge your progress

## Browser Compatibility

Works best in modern browsers:
- Chrome/Edge
- Firefox
- Safari
- Opera

## Need Help?

This is a static website with no backend. All functionality works offline after the initial load.
